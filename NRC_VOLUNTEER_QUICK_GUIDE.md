# 📋 NRC Volunteer Quick Guide - Adding Nominees

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Access the Form
Go to: `/get-involved/nrc-volunteer/nominees/add`

### Step 2: Select Award Tier
Choose one of three super categories:
1. **Africa Icon Blue Garnet Award** (Highest tier - Lifetime impact)
2. **Blue Garnet & Gold Certificate Awards** (Major contributions)
3. **Platinum Certificate of Recognition Awards** (Special recognition)

### Step 3: Select Award Category
The dropdown will show only categories under your selected tier.

### Step 4: Select Subcategory
Choose the specific subcategory that best fits your nominee.

### Step 5: Fill Nominee Details

#### Required Fields:
- ✅ Full Name / Organization Name
- ✅ Country
- ✅ Region/State
- ✅ Super Award Category
- ✅ Award Category
- ✅ Subcategory
- ✅ Achievement Summary (min 100 characters)
- ✅ Impact Metrics (min 50 characters)
- ✅ SDG Alignment (select at least 1)
- ✅ AU Agenda 2063 Alignment (min 20 characters)
- ✅ ESG Alignment (min 20 characters)
- ✅ Profile Image (required!)

#### Optional Fields:
- Organization Name
- Email
- Phone
- Website
- LinkedIn Profile
- Number of Beneficiaries
- Years of Impact
- Verification Links
- Media Links
- Additional Notes
- Supporting Documents

### Step 6: Upload Files
- **Profile Image**: Required - High-quality photo of nominee
- **Supporting Documents**: Optional - Additional proof/evidence

### Step 7: Submit
- Click "Save as Draft" to save and continue later
- Click "Submit for Review" to send for admin verification

## Tips for Success

### Writing Achievement Summary:
✅ **Do**:
- Be specific with numbers and impact
- Mention years of service
- Highlight unique contributions
- Use clear, concise language
- Include measurable outcomes

❌ **Don't**:
- Use vague statements
- Exaggerate without proof
- Copy from other sources
- Use less than 100 characters
- Leave out key achievements

### Example Good Summary:
> "Founded XYZ Foundation in 2010, providing scholarships to over 5,000 underprivileged students across 15 African countries. Built 25 schools in rural communities, trained 500+ teachers, and developed innovative STEM curriculum adopted by 100+ schools. Impact: 95% of scholarship recipients completed tertiary education."

### Example Bad Summary:
> "Great person who helps education in Africa. Very good work."

## After Submission

### What Happens Next:
1. **Immediate**: Nominee saved with status "REVIEW"
2. **Within 7 days**: Admin reviews submission
3. **If Approved**: Status changes to "VERIFIED"
4. **Public Display**: Nominee appears on public website automatically
5. **Notification**: You receive confirmation (if email provided)

### Where Your Nominee Appears:
- Public award category page
- Subcategory nominee list
- Searchable database
- Award gala materials (if selected)

### Tracking Your Nominations:
- Go to: `/get-involved/nrc-volunteer/dashboard`
- View all your submissions
- Check status (Draft, Review, Verified, Rejected, Published)
- See AGC rewards earned

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I nominate the same person for multiple categories?**
A: Yes, but each nomination must be submitted separately with category-specific achievements.

**Q: What if I don't have a profile image?**
A: Profile image is required. Use a professional photo or organization logo.

**Q: How long does verification take?**
A: Typically 3-7 business days. Complex cases may take longer.

**Q: Can I edit after submission?**
A: Drafts can be edited. Submitted nominations cannot be edited - contact admin if changes needed.

**Q: What are AGC rewards?**
A: Africa Garnet Coins - earned for verified nominations. Can be used for certificates and other benefits.

**Q: Can I nominate myself?**
A: Yes, self-nominations are allowed. Be objective and provide verifiable information.
